Research limitations identified:

- Individual contract validation does not verify graph node membership.
- Individual contract validation does not enforce permitted graph transitions.
- Individual contract validation does not enforce unique edge identifiers across contracts.
- Additional top-level and condition fields are accepted.
- Edge identifiers are optional.

These limitations have been examined experimentally but have not all been addressed in the production validator.

4. NATURAL-LANGUAGE AND LLM EXPERIMENTS
==================================================

[COMPLETED] Ollama-based structured contract generation experiments

[COMPLETED] Direct LLM routing comparison experiments

[COMPLETED] Natural-language variation experiments

[COMPLETED] Routing timing experiments

[COMPLETED] End-to-end timing experiment

Reported findings:

- Structured contract generation: 20/20 correct in the tested cases.
- Direct LLM routing: 50% expected-route accuracy across the tested cases.
- Direct LLM outputs were repeatable within the tested scenarios.
- Two separate natural-language variation experiments reported 80% and 90% accuracy.

Important qualifications:

- The contract generation and direct LLM routing experiments were different tasks.
- Their accuracy results must not be treated as a controlled head-to-head comparison.
- Deterministic routing timing excludes language-model contract generation.
- Timing results should not be interpreted as a complete end-to-end speed advantage without accounting for the full pipeline.
